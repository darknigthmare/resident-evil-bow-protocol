Raccoon City Zombie (Resident Evil Requiem)

Zombies make a glorious comeback in Resident Evil Requiem. These zombies are the poor residents of Raccoon City who succumbed to the original T-Virus infection that plagued the city, and then had the further misfortune to become infected with the mutated T-Virus strain. They will attack you with not only their bare hands, but anything they can get their hands on that could be used as a weapon including makeshift spears and various forms of debris.
