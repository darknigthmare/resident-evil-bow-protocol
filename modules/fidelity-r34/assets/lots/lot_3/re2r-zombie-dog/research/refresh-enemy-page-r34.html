Zombie Dog (Resident Evil 2 Remake)

These canines have been infected with the T-Virus, resulting in them becoming zombified. This has not restricted their ability to move incredibly fast and agile, and they will pursue you relentlessly. They do not take much ammunition to put down, but their speed can make them difficult to target.
