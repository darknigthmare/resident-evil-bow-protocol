Villager (Resident Evil 4 Remake)

These are the residents of the village that became infected with the Las Plagas parasite, and are now forced to do the bidding of their master, Osmund Saddler.

The villagers are capable of wielding many different types of weapons, primarily fashioning themselves with various agricultural tools to use as instruments in which to attack you.
