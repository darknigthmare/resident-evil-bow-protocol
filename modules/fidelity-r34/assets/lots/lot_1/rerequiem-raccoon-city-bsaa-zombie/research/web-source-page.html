Raccoon City BSAA Zombie (Resident Evil Requiem)

Zombies make a glorious comeback in Resident Evil Requiem. These zombies are were former members of the BSAA who were sent to Raccoon City to attempt to retrieve Elpis. They were inadvertently infected with the T-Virus, and now they roam the streets still wielding the weaponry they came armed with. These zombies can be especially tough to put down as not only do they come equipped with weapons and grenades, but they wear thick body armor that usually means you have to aim carefully for headshots or other exposed areas of the body.
