Licker (Resident Evil 2 Remake)

These aggressive creatures are the result of bio-weapon research conducted using the T-Virus. They are capable of climbing on walls and ceilings using their immensely large and powerful claws. Their weakness is their inability to see, however their hearing has developed to be incredibly sensitive.
Category Enemy
