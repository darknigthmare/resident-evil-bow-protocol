Zombie Lioness (Resident Evil Outbreak File #2)

The type of Zombie Lions encountered most frequently in the Raccoon City Zoo are mane-less female lions imported from Africa and South America. Most of their attacks can instantly drive you to the ground. If a Zombie Lioness attacks when a player character is weak enough, it may seize him or her by the throat and fling them across the area, causing instant death. But even the weakest attacks of this powerful creature inflict severe damage.
