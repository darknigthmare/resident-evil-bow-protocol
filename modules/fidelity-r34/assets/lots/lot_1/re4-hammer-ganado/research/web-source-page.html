Hammer Ganado (Resident Evil 4)

These are a larger variant of the Soldier Ganado. They do not carry projectile weapons, but instead choose to wield a large spiked hammer that causes a lot of damage if you get struck with it. In addition to this, these foes also wear metal body armor, making them a formidable enemy to take down. Aim for the head!
