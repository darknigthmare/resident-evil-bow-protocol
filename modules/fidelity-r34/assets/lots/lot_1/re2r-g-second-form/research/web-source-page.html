G (Second Form) (Resident Evil 2 Remake)

This is the secondary mutation of the G-Virus infected William Birkin. Damage sustained to a creature infected with the G-Virus triggers a surge of uncontrolled mutations. Despite being in the early stages of mutation, practically all recognisable features of the former human have been lost, even the host's head has been replaced, with William's tormented face now melded into this monster's chest.
