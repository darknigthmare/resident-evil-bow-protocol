Chunk (Resident Evil Requiem)

This monstrous enemy is the result of some of the grotesque experiments that occurred at the Rhodes Hill Chronic Care Center. There are two Chunks you encounter and they are in fact brothers. According to reports, after a surgical procedure to suppress the satiety center of the brain, these subject experienced an increase in appetite and weight gain. You can read more about their origin in the Satiety Suppression Medical Records file.
