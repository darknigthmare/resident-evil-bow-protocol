              <div class="paragraph">El Gigante is a failed by-product of Los Illuminados&#039; research. The rapid golem-like monster can barely distinguish friend from foe. The giant himself is invulnerable; you can only defeat him by killing the parasite on his back. You need to pelt El Gigante with shots until he holds his head in pain and drops to his knees, after which the parasite will emerge from its spine to defend the host. Attack the parasite before it disappears.</div>
